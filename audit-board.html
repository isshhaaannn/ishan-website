References worth stealing from.

Chosen against a specific problem in this archive rather than for general good looks.

exoape.com The smooth

The benchmark for scroll feel and image reveal. Nothing moves faster than the eye wants. This is the physics to copy.

studio-size.com The ground

Off-white, hairline rules, mono captions, Helvetica doing all the work. Closest existing site to the palette and type you picked.

basement.studio The index

A work list that stays typographic until you hover, then floods with image. Solves how to hold 18 clients without a wall of thumbnails.

are.na The density

How to show hundreds of images and keep it calm. Directly relevant to the 453.

darkroom.engineering The transitions

Page to page motion that never reloads the mood. Worth studying for how a case study opens and closes.

oddcommon.com The case study

Leads with the artefact, keeps the writing to a caption. The model for keeping copy light while the work carries it.

minhpham.design The solo

One designer, not a studio, presented with total confidence. The right scale of ambition for this build.

antinomy.studio The restraint

Proof that minimal does not mean empty. Useful as a brake when the grid starts getting clever.

pentagram.com The volume

A filterable index over an enormous body of work. The structural answer to browsing 62 projects.

The one thing no reference solves

Almost everything in this archive is a carousel, 8 to 16 slides deep. Every site above shows single images. The slide sequence is the format this portfolio lives or dies on, and it is the piece we design ourselves rather than borrow.

Before we build.

  • Francis is not on Google Fonts. Send the font files or a link to the exact cut you mean, otherwise the display face falls back to Helvetica.
  • Helvetica renders true on Mac and falls back to Arial on Windows. Licensing Helvetica Now for the web is the fix if that matters to you.
  • Confirm the five rooms above, or tell me which merges you disagree with.
  • Some client names are placeholders in the Drive, like christian and Banners. Send the real names or we leave them out.
